# Code of conduct

Nexlm is built by people in different countries, at different levels of experience, working on
software that handles other people's money. These are the rules for taking part.

## What we expect

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Review the change, not the contributor.
- Assume the person on the other side is doing their best with the context they have.
- Explain the "why" when you disagree, especially in review — a contributor can't act on "no".
- Respect that English is a second language for many contributors. Ask rather than assume.
- Keep trade disputes out of the repo. Issues are for the software.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's background, nationality, or identity
- Sharing someone's private information — trader identity, bank details, ID numbers, or messages from a trade chat
- Deliberately introducing code that weakens escrow, leaks data, or hides behaviour from review
- Using the project's channels to promote scams, "investment" schemes, or off-platform trades

## Reporting

Email **conduct@nexlm.app**. Reports go to the maintainers and stay private.
Tell us what happened, where, and who was involved — links help.

We'll acknowledge within 3 working days. Depending on what we find, we may ask for a change in
behaviour, edit or remove content, or block an account from the project. Deliberately malicious
code is reported to GitHub and, where money was at risk, disclosed to affected traders.

## Scope

This applies in the repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any Nexlm channel,
and to public behaviour where someone is representing the project.
